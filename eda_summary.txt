KEY FINDINGS
1. Revenue Jan-Nov 2011 vs Jan-Nov 2010: -1.4%; active customers: +1.0% - the business is essentially flat year on year.
2. Best month: Nov 2010 (1,155,978); weakest full month: Feb 2011 (442,294).
3. Average order value is stable at about 459 (range 399 - 557).
4. Sep-Nov accounted for 36% of all 2010 revenue (pre-Christmas stocking season).
5. Peak month: Nov in 2010 and Nov in 2011; quietest month of 2010: Feb.
6. Busiest weekday: Thu; busiest hour: 12:00. 78% of revenue is booked between 10:00 and 15:59.
7. Days with no sales at all: none.
8. The UK generates 83% of revenue and 91% of customers; the next markets are EIRE, Netherlands, Germany, France.
9. Among countries with 5+ customers, Netherlands has the highest revenue per customer (24,990 vs 2,625 for the UK).
10. The top 20% of products generate 78% of revenue; 1,016 of 4,618 products (22%) make up 80% of revenue.
11. 17% of products were bought by fewer than 10 customers (a long tail that matters for the recommender).
12. 28% of customers placed only one order; the median customer placed 3 orders and the most active placed 373.
13. The top 10% of customers generate 63% of revenue.
14. The median order is worth 303 but the mean is 459: a few very large (wholesale) orders pull the average up.
15. Time between purchases: median 32 days, 75% within 70 days, 90% within 148 days.
16. On average 80% of monthly active customers are returning customers, so the business depends on repeat buyers.
17. Average cohort retention: month 1 = 20%, month 3 = 22%, month 6 = 18%, month 12 = 18%.
18. Returns equal 2.8% of sales value (2.6% without the single largest cancelled invoice, C550456).
19. 42% of customers have at least one return; 5.1% of all customers return more than 10% of what they buy.
