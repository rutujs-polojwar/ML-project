1. Model explained: Gradient Boosting (sklearn) (RETRAINED copy of the Step 4 models); test snapshot 01 Jul 2011, 4,313 customers; typical churn probability 0.48.
2. Most important features (mean |SHAP|, pp of churn probability): Recency 5.2, Tenure 4.4, Frequency 3.6, PurchaseRate 3.6, AvgGapDays 2.8.
3. Most important behaviour groups: Recency and purchase rhythm 8.8 pp, Frequency and loyalty 7.4 pp, Spend and basket size 4.3 pp.
4. SHAP ranking agrees with permutation importance (Spearman 0.61); logistic regression agrees on direction for 11 of 17 features with a clear direction.
5. Recency: The higher the time since the last purchase, the higher the churn risk. Customers with Recency 228 or more: +8.7 pp; with 36 or less: -6.0 pp.
6. Tenure: The higher the customer age (days since first purchase), the higher the churn risk. Customers with Tenure 515 or more: +6.8 pp; with 241 or less: -0.4 pp. CAUTION: Tenure counts days since the first purchase inside the dataset (which starts in Dec 2009), so it is cut off at about 13 months in the 2010 training snapshots; see section 10.2.
7. Frequency: The higher the number of orders, the lower the churn risk. Customers with Frequency 7 or more: -8.0 pp; with 2 or less: +1.8 pp.
8. PurchaseRate: The higher the orders per 30 days, the lower the churn risk. Customers with PurchaseRate 0.57 or more: -3.3 pp; with 0.18 or less: +6.1 pp.
9. AvgGapDays: The higher the average gap between orders, the higher the churn risk. Customers with AvgGapDays 122 or more: +1.1 pp; with 42.16 or less: -3.3 pp. Customers with no purchase-gap history (1,091): +3.0 pp.
10. Customers with 1 order: main drivers Recency and PurchaseRate.
11. Customers with 2 orders: main drivers Recency and PurchaseRate.
12. Customers with 3-4 orders: main drivers Tenure and Recency.
13. Customers with 5+ orders: main drivers Frequency and Tenure.
14. Caution - Tenure: SHAP rank 2 but permutation rank 19; 48% of test customers are older than any training customer (training max 395 days, test max 577); diagnostic test ROC-AUC 0.775 with Tenure vs 0.784 without. Any decision to drop it belongs in Step 4 using training snapshots only.
15. Calculation checks: additivity error 4e-16; exact check on the logistic regression error 7e-16; sampling noise 0.27 pp per value, 0.004 pp in global averages.
